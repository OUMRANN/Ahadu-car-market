Common Questions

Before you ask, we answer.

Who is eligible for financing? +
Most salaried employees and registered business owners with 6+ months of income history qualify through one of our partner banks. We handle the paperwork with you in-store or online.
What does the 90-day guarantee cover? +
Any mechanical fault present at the time of sale that our inspection missed is repaired free of charge for 90 days or 5,000 km, whichever comes first.
Do you deliver outside of Addis Ababa? +
Yes — we deliver nationwide to 11 cities including Adama, Hawassa, Bahir Dar, and Mekelle, typically within 48 hours of signing.
Do you accept part-exchange on my current car? +
Yes. Ahadu appraises your current vehicle in-house and credits its value directly toward a vehicle from our inventory — we buy it as part of the deal, you never have to sell it yourself.
Can I see the full inspection report before buying? +
Every vehicle page includes a downloadable 175-point report with photos, so you know exactly what you're buying before you visit.
